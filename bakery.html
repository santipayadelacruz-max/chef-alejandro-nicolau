The Bakery

Bread, viennoiserie and pastry, baked fresh from Alejandro's own home oven. A quieter side of the kitchen, and one of the most requested.

The Oven

Baked Daily, Never Rushed

Every kitchen Alejandro runs keeps a living sourdough starter and a home oven working from early morning — long-fermented loaves, laminated pastry, and viennoiserie made the slow, traditional way.

What began as bread for the family table has become one of the most requested parts of the service: a basket at breakfast, a loaf sent home with guests, or a full bakery table for a gala reception.
