Hiring a Spanish Private Chef in London

By Alejandro Nicolau, Private Chef·Published 1 Oct 2026·Updated 11 Oct 2026

London has no shortage of chefs who can cook a version of Spanish food — but there's a real difference between a menu inspired by Spain and one built by someone who trained there, in the kitchens where the techniques were actually formed.

Why training in Spain matters

Spanish cooking, done properly, is built on technique that's easy to imitate and hard to master — the timing of a good paella socarrat, the balance of a proper pintxo, the confidence to let quality ingredients speak for themselves rather than over-complicating a dish. Training in San Sebastián, one of the world's most respected culinary regions, shapes how that technique is understood and applied.

What an authentic menu actually includes

A genuine Spanish private chef menu goes beyond the familiar patatas bravas and croquetas — though those have their place — into proper regional dishes: an authentic paella cooked the traditional way, a full spread of pintxos, and a tasting menu that tells a real story about Spanish food culture. The Spanish Heritage page sets out exactly what this looks like.

Bringing it to a London event

Whether it's a full Spanish-themed dinner party, a paella-led garden event, or a single course within a broader tasting menu, a Spanish private chef in London brings both the technical skill and the cultural understanding to make it feel authentic rather than a themed approximation.

Who this suits

Clients planning a celebration with Spanish heritage of their own, anyone who has fallen in love with the food while travelling in Spain, or hosts simply looking for something different from the usual London dinner party circuit all tend to find this the right fit.

Pairing it with the right drinks

A proper Spanish menu deserves a proper drinks pairing — sherry, cava, or a well-made cocktail list alongside the food, rather than an afterthought.

Why This Resonates Particularly With Clients With Spanish Ties

For clients with their own connection to Spain — through family, marriage, or simply years of holidaying there — an authentically cooked Spanish menu carries meaning that goes beyond the food itself. It's often described as the closest thing to bringing a specific memory or place back to life at the table. This is part of why Spanish-themed private dining has become such a popular choice for milestone celebrations among clients with that personal connection to the cuisine and culture.

Combining Spanish Cuisine With Other Elements of Your Event

A Spanish menu doesn't need to stand alone — it can be paired with flamenco-inspired music, Spanish wine and sherry service, or décor that nods to the theme, creating a fully immersive evening rather than just an authentically cooked meal on its own.

Bringing a Piece of Spain to Your Table

Whatever the occasion, an authentically cooked Spanish menu offers something genuinely different from the usual London dinner party circuit — a chance to share a real culinary tradition with your guests, cooked by someone who trained within it.

A Growing Appetite for Regional Spanish Cuisine

London's dining scene has increasingly moved beyond generic "Spanish-style" tapas toward a genuine appreciation for specific regional traditions — Basque pintxos, Valencian paella, Andalusian sherry culture. A private chef trained specifically within these traditions is well positioned to meet this growing interest with real authenticity, rather than a generalised interpretation of Spanish food.

Questions specific to Spanish cooking

If you want a Spanish evening, ask about regional focus, such as Basque, Catalan or Andalusian, and whether paella is cooked live. Check which ingredients are imported and how drinks pairings are handled. A chef with real Spanish training can explain the origin of each dish and adapt it to your guests, while keeping the flavours recognisably authentic. That depth of knowledge is what separates a themed menu from a genuinely Spanish one.
