Private chef meal prep sits somewhere between hiring a chef for a one-off event and using a generic meal delivery service — and understanding how it actually works helps decide if it's the right fit for your routine.

01How the process starts

It begins with a conversation about goals, preferences, and any dietary requirements or macro targets you're working toward. This shapes the kind of menu built for you, whether that's general balanced eating or something more structured around specific nutrition goals.

02A typical week

Meals are cooked fresh, usually once or twice a week, in batches that cover several days — proteins, vegetables, and carbohydrates prepared and portioned so you have grab-and-go meals rather than daily cooking. The Meal Prep menu shows the kind of dishes typically included.

03Where it happens

Meal prep is usually cooked in your own kitchen, using your fridge and storage, so meals are fresh and ready exactly where you'll eat them — rather than shipped and reheated from an external kitchen.

04How it differs from a meal delivery subscription

A private chef meal prep service is built around you specifically — your preferences, your macros, and any dishes you genuinely enjoy — rather than a fixed rotation of menu options designed for a broad customer base.

05Who it suits

Busy professionals who want to eat well without daily cooking, anyone working toward specific fitness or nutrition goals, and households who simply want consistently good, balanced food without the weekly mental load of planning it themselves.

06What Happens if Your Schedule Changes Week to Week

Life rarely stays perfectly consistent, and a good meal prep arrangement builds in flexibility for that — adjusting delivery days, pausing during travel, or shifting portion counts if guests are staying. Rather than a rigid subscription that penalises any change to your routine, an ongoing private chef meal prep relationship tends to work more like an adaptable, personal service that flexes around your actual life rather than the other way around.

07Getting the Most Out of the First Few Weeks

The first few weeks of a meal prep arrangement are the best time to give honest feedback — which dishes you loved, which didn't quite land, any portion sizes that felt off. This early feedback loop is what allows the ongoing plan to be fine-tuned specifically to your actual preferences.

08Making Healthy Eating Easier

At its best, a private chef meal prep service removes the daily friction of eating well, replacing it with genuinely enjoyable, ready-to-go meals that fit around your life rather than adding to its demands.

09A Sustainable Long-Term Habit

Many clients who start with meal prep for a specific short-term goal find they simply continue the arrangement afterwards, having discovered how much easier consistently eating well becomes once the planning and cooking is no longer their own daily responsibility.

10A Final Encouragement

If the idea of consistently eating well without the daily effort appeals to you, there's rarely a wrong time to start — most clients wish they'd begun sooner once they experience how much easier it makes their week.

11What a first visit looks like

A first meal prep visit usually begins with a short consultation about tastes, dietary needs and how many meals you want covered. The chef then shops, arrives at your home on the agreed day and cooks for several hours in your kitchen. Meals are portioned, labelled and stored with reheating guidance, and the kitchen is left clean. From the second week onwards the menu can be adjusted as you give feedback, so the plan gets closer to your preferences each time rather than repeating the same rotation.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How often does the chef come to prepare meals?

It is flexible and depends on your needs. Private chef meal prep is typically once or twice a week, with each visit covering several days of meals.

Can meal prep be built around specific macro targets?

Yes. Meal prep can be planned around protein, calorie and macro goals, similar to the approach shown in Alejandro Nicolau's Balanced High-Protein Meal Prep menu.
