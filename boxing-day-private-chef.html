Boxing Day Private Chef London

The second feast, without the second shift

A relaxed Boxing Day lunch or supper cooked at home, with a cold table, a lighter feast or a proper second celebration.

Enquire Now All Occasions

Boxing Day is the open-house day of the British year: people drop in, stay for hours and graze. It is also when hosts are most tired, with the fridge full of leftovers and the cooking from the day before still on the surfaces.

A private chef makes it easy. Alejandro can build a cold table around the best of the festive leftovers, cook a lighter second feast, or do something completely different. The menu is planned with you, shopping and cooking are taken care of, and the kitchen is left clean.

Boxing Day: key facts

Alejandro Nicolau is a London-based private chef who cooks Boxing Day menus fresh in the client's own home, with shopping, cooking, service and the kitchen left clean, across London and the United Kingdom.

The menu

Boxing Day menu ideas

01A proper cold table

Glazed ham, cold roast beef, smoked salmon, pickles, chutneys and good bread. Relaxed, generous and designed for grazing over several hours.

02Turkey and ham, reimagined

Pies, croquetas, sandwiches and salads made from festive meats, giving leftovers a second life that tastes intentional.

03Soups and hot dishes

A warming soup, a baked pasta or a slow-cooked stew that can sit on the stove for guests who arrive at different times.

04Cheese and charcuterie

A generous board with fruit, nuts and chutneys, plus bread and crackers. Easy to eat standing up.

05Light and bright

After a heavy Christmas, a lighter lunch with fish, salads and citrus desserts is increasingly popular.

06Bubbles and brunch drinks

Bloody Marys, Bucks Fizz and a simple cocktail can be served by a bartender if the day is a larger gathering.

Planning

Planning Boxing Day

01Decide what you want to keep

If you have leftover turkey or ham, tell us. The chef can work them into the menu, or start fresh if you prefer.

02Make it a sit-down or a drop-in

A fixed lunch time suits a seated table. An open-house window suits a buffet that guests can visit at their own pace.

03Keep it simple for you

Setup, serving and clearing are handled, so as a host you are with your guests all day.

04Book early

Boxing Day is a Saturday in 2026, so it is popular. Ask in November for the best choice.

Tips

Boxing Day hosting tips

01Tell us what is left

If you have leftover turkey or ham, say so and we can plan around it.

02Keep it informal

Boxing Day works best as a drop-in. A buffet suits people arriving at different times.

03Choose lighter food

After Christmas Day many guests want something fresher. Salads and fish are very welcome.

04Plan the clear-up

The kitchen is cleaned before the chef leaves, so you do not start the new day with Christmas dishes.

Questions

Boxing Day Private Chef: FAQs

Do you cook on Boxing Day?

Availability on 26 December is limited. Ask early and we will confirm whether a booking is possible.

Can you use our Christmas leftovers?

Yes, where it makes sense. The chef can plan dishes with leftovers, but will tell you honestly if something is better made fresh.

Can you do a buffet for a large open house?

Yes. A buffet or grazing table is a natural fit for Boxing Day, and staff can be added for larger groups.

Is a lighter menu possible?

Yes. Fish, salads and citrus desserts are a popular way to follow a heavy Christmas Day.
