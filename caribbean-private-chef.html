Caribbean Private Chef

Bold, sun-ripe flavour, done properly

Bold, confident Caribbean flavour — proper jerk technique and genuinely ripe, sun-grown produce, not a diluted version.

Enquire Now Explore the Menus

Caribbean cooking is built on bold, confident flavour — proper jerk seasoning with real heat and depth, genuinely ripe tropical produce, and a rich mix of African, indigenous and colonial influences across the islands. Alejandro's Caribbean menus take that boldness seriously rather than toning it down, while still calibrating spice to suit whoever's actually at the table.

Caribbean private chef at a glance

Jerk, done properly

Proper jerk seasoning is built from a genuine blend of scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme and other aromatics, marinated and cooked with real care rather than relying on a shortcut spice mix — the difference comes through clearly in the final dish.

Island variety

Caribbean cooking varies meaningfully from island to island, and a menu can lean toward a specific island's traditions — Jamaican, Trinidadian, Barbadian — or draw more broadly across the wider Caribbean, depending on preference.

Fresh, ripe produce

Tropical fruit and vegetables used across a Caribbean menu are chosen for genuine ripeness and quality, reflecting the sun-grown produce that defines the cuisine at its best, rather than a pale, underripe substitute.

What's included

A Caribbean booking includes menu planning around island preference and spice level, all shopping and sourcing of properly ripe produce, on-site cooking and service, and a complete clean-up before the team leaves.

Suited to a summer garden event

Caribbean cuisine's bold, outdoor-friendly style makes it a popular choice for a summer garden party, with jerk and grilled dishes particularly well suited to an outdoor setting.

Rum & drinks pairing

A Caribbean menu pairs naturally with rum-based cocktails, and a drinks pairing can be planned alongside the food through the existing Bar & Cocktail Hire service for a fuller island-themed evening.

A menu for a celebratory summer event

Caribbean cuisine's bold, outdoor-friendly character makes it a particularly strong choice for a milestone summer birthday or a larger garden celebration with a genuinely festive atmosphere.

A tropical, properly made dessert

Caribbean desserts lean into genuinely ripe tropical fruit and rich spices like nutmeg and allspice, giving the end of the meal the same warmth and boldness as everything that came before it.

Booking a Caribbean menu

An enquiry through the contact form, with any island preference you have in mind, starts the planning process, with a full proposal confirmed within 24 hours.

Scaling to your event

A Caribbean menu is planned to suit any guest count, from a small family dinner through to a larger summer garden celebration, with the kitchen team scaled to match.

Equipment & setup

Where outdoor grilling isn't possible, the team adapts jerk and other grilled dishes to a standard kitchen setup without losing the dish's core character, so no specialist equipment is required from the host.

A menu for any time of year

While particularly popular for a summer garden event, a Caribbean menu works just as well as a warming indoor dinner during the colder months, leaning more on stewed and braised dishes from the same tradition.

Drinks to match

A good rum punch or a simple rum flight suits a Caribbean menu particularly well, and Alejandro can suggest a drinks pairing to run alongside the courses, from the first jerk-spiced bite through to dessert.

How it's presented

Dishes are plated with the same bold colour and generosity the cuisine is known for, without losing the clean, considered presentation a private dining table calls for.

Make it your own

Heat level and the balance between seafood-led and jerk-led courses are agreed with you in advance, so the final menu reflects your own taste rather than a single fixed version of the cuisine.
