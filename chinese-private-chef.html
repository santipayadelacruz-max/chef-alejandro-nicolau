The details

What to expect

01Regional variety

Menus can draw on a specific regional tradition — Cantonese cooking's lighter, more delicate technique, Sichuan's bold use of chilli and numbing peppercorn, or a broader tasting menu moving across several regions.

02Wok technique, done properly

High-heat wok cooking is a genuine skill, and dishes relying on it are prepared with the right equipment and technique to get a proper sear and texture — something that's genuinely difficult to replicate in a standard home kitchen without the right setup.

03A proper multi-course format

A Chinese tasting menu, moving through a sequence of smaller dishes — some steamed, some stir-fried, some braised — gives guests a genuine tour through different textures and techniques across the evening, rather than a handful of large sharing plates.

04What's included

A Chinese booking includes a planning conversation covering regional style and spice preference, all shopping and sourcing, on-site cooking with proper wok technique where needed, and a complete clean-up before the team leaves.

05Suited to a celebratory gathering

Chinese cuisine's tradition of shared, varied dishes makes it a natural fit for a larger celebratory gathering, such as a significant birthday or a festive family occasion.

06A menu for a significant celebration

Certain Chinese dishes carry genuine symbolic weight at a celebration — a particular preparation for a birthday or a New Year gathering, for instance — and these can be worked into the menu specifically on request.

07Tea pairing

Where a client wants it, a simple tea pairing can be planned alongside the meal, drawing on the same tradition of balancing rich dishes with something lighter between courses.

08A considered finish to the meal

Dessert in a Chinese menu is typically lighter than in some Western traditions — fresh fruit, a simple sweet soup, or a small pastry course — chosen to close the meal without overwhelming what came before it.

09Booking a Chinese menu

An enquiry through the contact form, noting any regional preference, starts the planning conversation, with a full written proposal confirmed within 24 hours.

10Scaling to your event

A Chinese menu is planned to suit any guest count, from an intimate dinner through to a larger celebratory gathering, with the number of dishes and kitchen team scaled accordingly.

11Equipment & setup

The team brings the specialist wok and steaming equipment a proper Chinese menu calls for, so a standard home kitchen is all that's needed from the host.

12Drinks to match

Tea is offered as a traditional accompaniment alongside wine or beer, and Alejandro can suggest a pairing structure — lighter teas or whites with delicate steamed dishes, something with more weight alongside braised or smoked courses.

13How it's presented

Courses are brought to the table in the traditional sequence rather than all at once, so the rhythm of a shared Chinese meal is preserved even in a plated, multi-course format.

14Make it your own

Menus are built around your own preferences from the first conversation — more Cantonese-leaning and delicate, or bolder and Sichuan-forward with more chilli and numbing spice — rather than offered as a single fixed format every client receives.

Questions

Chinese Private Chef: FAQs

Can the menu focus on a specific regional style, like Sichuan or Cantonese?

Yes. A Chinese menu from Alejandro Nicolau can focus on a specific regional tradition such as Sichuan or Cantonese, agreed during the initial planning conversation.

Is proper wok technique used, or is this a simplified version?

Proper wok technique. Alejandro Nicolau's team uses the right equipment and high heat for dishes that call for it, rather than a simplified home-kitchen version.

Can the spice level be adjusted for Sichuan-style dishes?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau calibrates the chilli and numbing-pepper intensity of Sichuan-style dishes to your table during planning, so the heat suits your guests.

Is dim sum available as part of a Chinese booking?

Yes. Steamed and fried dim sum can be included in a Chinese tasting menu from Alejandro Nicolau, prepared fresh as part of the booking.
