Cookie Policy

What cookies this website uses, why, and how you can change your choice at any time.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help a site work, remember your choices and, with your permission, understand how it is used so it can be improved.

Analytics and your consent

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are visited and how people find the site. Analytics cookies are only set if you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you choose Decline, or do nothing, analytics storage stays switched off. We do not use advertising or marketing cookies.

Change or withdraw your choice

You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page, which shows the banner again. You can also delete cookies or block them in your browser settings; the website will keep working, though your choice may be asked again.

Other services on this site

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads. The contact form sends your enquiry through Formspree, and the WhatsApp button opens a conversation on WhatsApp. These services have their own privacy policies and are not controlled by this website.
