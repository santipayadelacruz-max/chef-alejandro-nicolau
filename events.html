Private Chef London Events

Alejandro Nicolau is a London private chef for events: private households, corporate events and gala dinners across the city, with his own team and the logistics to travel further when your event calls for it.

What We Do

We Cook for Big Events and Dinners

Private chef events in London, from intimate dinners and family celebrations to corporate receptions and gala dinners, with full cocktail and bar service, delivered by a team built to handle real scale. Available across the UK, with international travel by arrangement.

01 · London Private Household

Daily service, no exceptions

As a resident chef for households across London, Alejandro designs daily menus tailored to dietary requirements, entertains visiting guests, and maintains the highest standard of discretion.

Experience includes managing kosher-style cooking for a British Jewish family, with particular care for tradition and consistency.

Check availability
02 · Private & Corporate Events

One-off events, planned end to end

From intimate dinner parties to full corporate receptions, Alejandro and his team handle every detail: menu, service staff, and logistics, so the event runs exactly as planned.

Receptions, business lunches and company dinners are delivered with the same standard as a Michelin-starred restaurant, without leaving the office or venue.

Check availability
03 · Gala Dinners

High-society receptions

Luxury-hotel-level table settings, multi-course menus and full service coordination for private clients and high-profile receptions.

Every gala dinner is planned down to the last detail: tableware, pace of service, pairing, and the visual presentation of each dish.

Check availability
04 · International

His cooking, anywhere in the world

Based in London, Alejandro travels internationally with his own team and protocol, adapting purchasing and kitchen logistics to the particularities of each destination.

Ideal for destination weddings, family celebrations at a second home, or multi-day events.

Check availability
05 · Yacht charters

Booking a yacht instead?

Yacht and superyacht charters are handled separately, with itinerary-led menus and crew coordination. See the dedicated yacht chef page for that service. This page covers private chef events on land in London and the UK.

06 · Cocktail & Bar Service

A bar built around your evening

We can provide our own bar setup, personalised for your experience, from a welcome cocktail to a full evening's drinks service, run by our own bartenders.

We can also personalise and dress your table with our own equipment, so the whole event, food, drinks and table, feels considered from start to finish.
