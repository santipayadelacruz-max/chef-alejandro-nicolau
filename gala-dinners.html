Planning a gala menu

Gala menus are planned around the formality and scale of the occasion — typically a multi-course plated dinner, designed to be prepared and served consistently whether the room holds fifty guests or several hundred.

Full event staffing

A gala booking includes a complete kitchen and front-of-house team, scaled to the size of the event, coordinated under a single point of contact so there's no gap between the kitchen and the room.

Working with your venue or event planner

Gala dinners are often planned alongside an existing event planner or venue team, and the kitchen is set up to coordinate smoothly within that wider structure — confirming logistics, timing and any venue-specific requirements well ahead of the event.

Coordinating with AV, speeches & a running order

A gala dinner is rarely just a meal — speeches, awards or entertainment usually run alongside the food, and course timing is planned around that running order so plates are cleared and courses served at exactly the right moments rather than competing with the evening's programme.

Sourcing at scale without losing quality

Cooking a genuinely fine-dining standard menu for a large room requires sourcing and preparation to be planned well ahead of the event, with suppliers confirmed early enough that quality never has to be compromised simply because the guest count is high.

Staffing continuity across multi-night events

For galas that run across consecutive evenings, such as a multi-day conference or festival, the same kitchen and service team is kept in place throughout, so standards and timing stay consistent from the first night to the last rather than varying with a rotating crew.

Allergen management at scale

Managing allergens and dietary requirements across a large guest list requires more rigorous process than a smaller event — a clear system for recording requirements, flagging affected plates in the kitchen, and confirming each one before it leaves is treated as essential, not optional, at gala scale.

Rehearsals & timing run-throughs

For particularly large or complex galas, a timing run-through ahead of the event itself — confirming exactly when each course should be served relative to the evening's programme — helps the whole team, kitchen and front-of-house alike, execute the night with no surprises.

Branding & bespoke menu presentation

Where a gala is tied to a specific brand, charity or cause, menu cards and course presentation can be subtly tailored to reflect that branding, adding a further layer of polish to an evening built around a specific cause or company.

Designing the experience

A gala dinner is judged on the whole evening, not only the food. Alejandro Nicolau works with you on menu sequence, table dressing and the pace of service so guests move smoothly from reception to dessert, with moments of drama built in where they suit the occasion.

Coordinating a larger team

Larger galas rely on a clear plan for the kitchen and floor staff. Roles, timings and plating standards are agreed beforehand, so every table receives each course at the same standard and at the right moment.
