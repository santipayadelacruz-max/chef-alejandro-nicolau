Origins

Born in Spain in 1998.
Trained in kitchens that set the standard.

Alejandro began his career under the guidance of Martín Berasategui (3 Michelin stars), building a strong foundation in fine dining before developing expertise across a wide range of international cuisines. Since 2020, he has worked exclusively with UHNW families, private residences, and superyachts, based in London and available internationally.

Now based in London, Alejandro creates bespoke dining experiences, from daily family meals and intimate dinners with friends to celebrations and private events for hundreds of guests. He leads a trusted team of sous chefs, pastry chefs, bakers, front-of-house professionals, and drinks managers, ensuring every event is delivered with the same exceptional standard of service and attention to detail.

Private chef in London

One chef and one team, from a dinner for two to a gala for hundreds

Alejandro Nicolau is a private chef based in London who cooks in your own home, on board your yacht and at events across the UK and abroad. Every booking starts with a conversation about your guests, your space and the occasion, and ends with a menu built around them.

You can book him for a dinner party, a family celebration, a corporate event or a week of daily meals, and choose the style of cooking from more than a dozen cuisines. Chefs, bartenders and waiters can be added when the guest list grows, and the kitchen is always left clean. Browse the areas we cook in or send an enquiry and receive a tailored proposal within 24 hours.
