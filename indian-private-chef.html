The details

What to expect

01Spice, calibrated to your table

Spice level is discussed and calibrated during planning rather than assumed — a milder, more approachable menu for a mixed table, or something with real heat for guests who want it, built dish by dish rather than applied as a blanket setting.

02Regional variety

Menus can draw on a specific regional tradition — the richer, tandoor-led cooking of the north, the coconut and seafood-forward cooking of the south, or a broader mix spanning several regions across one tasting menu.

03A proper multi-course format

An Indian tasting menu, built from a sequence of smaller plates rather than a handful of large sharing dishes, gives guests a genuine tour through different textures and spice profiles across the evening.

04What's included

An Indian booking includes a full planning conversation covering regional preference and spice level, all shopping and ingredient sourcing, on-site cooking and service, and a complete clean-up before the team leaves.

05Suited to celebrations & everyday dining alike

An Indian menu works equally well for a significant family celebration and a more relaxed weekend dinner, with the format and number of courses scaled to suit your specific occasion.

06Bread & accompaniments, made fresh

Freshly made bread — naan, roti or paratha depending on the menu — is prepared on the day as part of a proper Indian booking, alongside fresh chutneys and raitas rather than shop-bought alternatives.

07A menu for a larger family gathering

Indian cuisine's tradition of generous, varied spreads makes it a natural fit for a larger multi-generational family celebration, with dishes planned to suit a genuinely wide range of ages and spice preferences at the same table.

08Dessert & the end of the meal

Indian desserts, from a proper kulfi to a warmly spiced rice pudding, bring the same attention to detail as the rest of the menu, chosen to round off the meal rather than feeling like an afterthought.

09Booking an Indian menu

An enquiry through the contact form, mentioning any regional preference and spice level you have in mind, starts the planning conversation, with a full proposal confirmed within 24 hours.

10Scaling to your event

An Indian menu is planned to suit any guest count, from a small family dinner through to a larger celebration, with the kitchen team and number of dishes scaled to match.

11Equipment & setup

Any specialist equipment an Indian menu calls for, including a tandoor-style setup where relevant, is brought by the team, so a standard home kitchen is all that's needed from the host.

12Drinks to match

Spice level and drinks pairing are discussed together, since a cooling lassi, a crisp lager or an off-dry white all work differently against a menu's heat. Alejandro adjusts the pairing suggestions to the specific dishes and spice level chosen.

13How it's presented

Dishes are plated individually rather than served from large shared trays, so a tasting menu reads as a proper multi-course dinner while still carrying the generosity that's central to Indian hospitality.

14From regional dishes to modern plates

Indian food is not one cuisine but many, from the tandoor cooking of the north to the coconut-led dishes of the south and the seafood of the coast. Menus can follow a single region closely or take inspiration from several and present them in a refined, modern way. Spice blends are built fresh, rice and breads are made to order, and heat is adjusted to your guests rather than set at one level for everyone.

15Looking after every guest at the table

Indian menus are naturally generous for vegetarians, vegans and guests who avoid certain ingredients, and many dishes are already gluten free. Share the dietary needs of your group when you enquire and the menu is designed so that everyone eats well, not just those without restrictions. Mild and hot versions of the same dish can sit side by side so mixed groups and families with children are comfortable.

16Where we cook

Alejandro cooks in private homes and venues across London, including Mayfair, Kensington, Chelsea, Knightsbridge, Belgravia, Notting Hill, Marylebone, Hampstead, St John's Wood, Wimbledon. He also travels for events across the United Kingdom, including Surrey, Windsor, Henley-on-Thames, the Cotswolds, Oxford, Cambridge, Brighton, Bath, Edinburgh, Manchester. You can see every area on the locations page, or send your address and date through the enquiry form to confirm availability.

Questions

Indian Private Chef: FAQs

Can the spice level be adjusted for a guest who doesn't like heat?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau sets the spice level dish by dish and calibrates it to your table, including separately for individual guests who prefer less heat.

Can the menu focus on a specific region of India?

Yes. An Indian menu from Alejandro Nicolau can focus on one regional tradition or span several regions, depending on your preference.

Is a vegetarian Indian menu available?

Yes. Indian cuisine has an exceptionally strong vegetarian tradition, so Alejandro Nicolau can cook a fully vegetarian Indian menu that does not feel like a reduced version of the full one.

Is this a tasting-menu format, or more traditional sharing-style service?

Both. Alejandro Nicolau can serve an Indian menu as a sequence of smaller tasting courses or as a traditional sharing-style spread, depending on what suits your event.
