Japanese & Sushi Private Chef

Omakase, cooked at your table

An omakase-style sushi experience and a full Japanese tasting menu, cooked and served directly at your own table.

Japanese cuisine rewards precision more than almost any other culinary tradition — the exact cut of a piece of fish, the precise seasoning of sushi rice, the timing of a dish served at exactly the right temperature. Alejandro's Japanese menus bring this same precision to a private setting, whether that's a full omakase-style sushi counter experience or a broader Japanese tasting menu moving beyond sushi into other courses.

Omakase, at your own table

An omakase format — where the chef selects each course based on the best available ingredients that day — is available as a private booking, bringing the same experience usually reserved for a high-end sushi counter directly into your home.

Beyond sushi

A full Japanese tasting menu can move well beyond sushi and sashimi alone, including grilled, simmered and other cooked courses that make up a complete, traditional Japanese dining experience.

Fish quality & sourcing

Sushi-grade fish is sourced specifically for each booking, with quality and freshness treated as non-negotiable given how much a sushi or sashimi course depends on exactly that.

What's included

A Japanese or sushi booking includes a full planning conversation covering format and course selection, sourcing of sushi-grade fish and other ingredients, on-site preparation and service, and a complete clean-up before the team leaves.

Suited to an intimate occasion

The precision and focus of an omakase-style experience suits a smaller, more intimate guest list particularly well, though a larger Japanese tasting menu is equally available for a bigger celebration.

Sake & drinks pairing

A sake pairing can be planned alongside an omakase or wider Japanese tasting menu, matched course by course as part of the same booking for a more complete experience.

Equipment & setup

The team brings the specialist equipment needed for proper sushi preparation, including the right knives and rice-cooking setup, so nothing beyond a standard kitchen is required from the host.

A traditional close to the meal

A Japanese meal traditionally finishes on something light rather than a heavy dessert — fresh seasonal fruit or a simple, delicate sweet — in keeping with the rest of the menu's sense of restraint and balance.

Booking a Japanese or sushi menu

An enquiry through the contact form, noting whether you'd like an omakase format or a wider tasting menu, starts the planning process, with a full proposal confirmed within 24 hours.

Scaling to your event

A Japanese or sushi menu is planned to suit any guest count, from an intimate omakase counter experience for two through to a larger tasting menu for a bigger celebration.

A menu for any occasion

A Japanese or sushi menu suits both an intimate, quiet celebration and a larger, more social gathering, with the format adjusted — a focused omakase counter experience, or a broader shared tasting menu — to match the occasion.

Seasonal fish selection

Fish selection for an omakase or sushi-led menu follows genuine seasonality, reflecting what's actually at its best at the time of year rather than a fixed, unchanging list of options.

Drinks to match

A good sake flight, chosen to match the specific fish and preparations on the night, is a natural accompaniment to an omakase-style menu, and Alejandro is glad to suggest a pairing structure alongside a chilled white if sake isn't preferred.

How it's presented

Nigiri and sashimi are cut and plated to order throughout service, immediately before each piece is served, which is the standard that distinguishes a proper omakase experience from one prepared in advance.
