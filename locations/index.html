Questions

Coverage and booking: FAQs

Which areas does Alejandro cover as a private chef?

Alejandro covers 112 areas across London and the UK, from Mayfair, Chelsea and Hampstead to the Cotswolds, the Solent, Edinburgh and the Scottish Highlands, and travels internationally for events and yachts.

Does Alejandro travel outside London for private chef bookings?

Yes. Alejandro and his team travel across the UK for dinners, weekends and events, and abroad for international clients and yachts. Travel is planned with you when the booking is confirmed.

My area is not listed. Can I still book a private chef?

Yes. Share your location, date and guest count through the enquiry form on the contact page and Alejandro will confirm availability for your area.

How do I check availability for my date and location?

Send the date, location, guest count and any dietary needs through the enquiry form on the contact page. A tailored proposal follows within 24 hours.
