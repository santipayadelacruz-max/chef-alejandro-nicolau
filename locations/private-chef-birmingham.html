Birmingham

Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

Birmingham's mix of city-centre apartments, canal-side conversions and larger houses in Edgbaston and Sutton Coldfield makes for a varied private dining brief, and Alejandro adapts the same fine-dining approach to each setting. The city's strong business and events calendar — from the NEC's exhibition schedule to a steady flow of corporate entertaining around the Colmore Row financial district — means a meaningful share of bookings here are client dinners and company celebrations, planned with the same discretion as any London corporate booking. Edgbaston itself, with its Victorian villas and leafy streets near the cricket ground, hosts a good number of family celebrations and milestone birthdays, often timed around the Edgbaston Test match in summer when the whole area is in a celebratory mood. Further out, the larger houses around Sutton Coldfield and the Four Oaks area give more room for a full garden event or a live-cooked paella station, weather permitting. Birmingham's own culinary identity — the balti triangle, a genuinely excellent curry scene, a growing fine-dining presence around Brindleyplace — isn't something Alejandro tries to compete with directly; instead, bookings here tend to lean into his own Mediterranean, Spanish and fusion menus as a contrast to what's already excellent locally, giving guests something distinct from their regular dining options. Transport into Birmingham is straightforward from London, whether by road or a fast train into New Street, so travel costs and logistics for a Birmingham booking are comparable to a weekend-countryside booking rather than a genuinely remote one. For clients hosting a larger event — a company's annual dinner, a milestone wedding anniversary, a significant birthday — the same full staffing, bar service and clean-up included in any London booking applies here, with the team travelling up for the day or staying over for a multi-day booking as needed. Dietary requirements, a request for a specific cuisine, or a particular format (plated dinner versus a more relaxed buffet-style evening) are all discussed during the initial enquiry exactly as they would be for a London client. Planning a Birmingham booking follows the same process as anywhere else — an initial enquiry covering date, guest count and occasion, followed by a short conversation about cuisine and any dietary needs, with a full written proposal confirmed within 24 hours. For a larger corporate event tied to a specific NEC exhibition date, slightly earlier enquiry helps secure the preferred date given how busy the city's calendar gets around major exhibition weeks.
