The details

What to expect in Birmingham

01Apartments, villas and suburbs

Birmingham has canal-side apartments, Victorian villas and large suburban houses. We confirm layout and plan menus accordingly.

02A city of many cuisines

Birmingham's food culture is global. Menus can draw on Indian, Middle Eastern, Japanese and Spanish flavours, alongside classical techniques.

03Business and celebrations

Client dinners, birthdays, weddings and festive parties are common bookings.

04Midlands produce

Warwickshire and Worcestershire fruit, vegetables and meat feature in seasonal menus.

05Travel to Birmingham

Birmingham is about an hour and a half from London by train.

06A sample menu for Birmingham

A menu inspired by the city's cuisines could include a spiced lamb kofta with yoghurt, a tandoori-style cod with herbs and a cardamom panna cotta.

07Birmingham's cosmopolitan table

Birmingham's restaurants span Indian, Pakistani, Chinese, Caribbean and Italian cooking, and its guests are used to bold flavours. We bring that awareness into private dining, with menus that can include Indian-inspired spices, Middle Eastern salads and Japanese techniques alongside classical European dishes.

Experience

Alejandro’s experience in Birmingham

Birmingham is a city of varied tastes, from canal-side conversions to leafy Edgbaston, and its hosts enjoy flavours from everywhere. Alejandro's menus are comfortable moving between Spanish, Mediterranean and Asian influences, so a mixed table of guests finds something to talk about.

Spanish by upbringing and Basque by training, Alejandro mixes warm hospitality with exact technique, which suits hosts and guests who know their food.
