Bournemouth

Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

Bournemouth's genuinely attractive seafront, its wide sandy beaches and the larger houses tucked into the surrounding Canford Cliffs and Branksome Park areas, give the town a quietly affluent character that's sometimes overlooked in favour of nearby Sandbanks, despite the two areas sitting almost side by side. Family celebrations and milestone birthdays held in one of Bournemouth's larger coastal properties make up the bulk of bookings here, often with sea views forming a natural backdrop to an evening that leans into fresh, seafood-forward menus drawing on the excellent catch available along the Dorset coast. Bournemouth's own events calendar, including a strong summer tourism season and occasional larger conferences through its international centre, brings a secondary flow of corporate and hospitality-related bookings too. The town's proximity to Sandbanks and Poole Harbour means Bournemouth clients are sometimes already familiar with Alejandro's coastal work nearby, and a Bournemouth booking is occasionally the start of an ongoing relationship that later extends to a yacht or marina-based event in the same part of the Dorset coast. As with any southern coastal booking, logistics from London are straightforward, with travel typically no more involved than a weekend-countryside booking elsewhere in the Home Counties. Menus here are planned around the same full consultation as anywhere else — guest numbers, dietary needs, preferred format — before being finalised ahead of the date. Bournemouth's long summer tourist season means local suppliers are well used to a high volume of quality produce moving through the area between May and September, which keeps ingredient sourcing straightforward even during the town's busiest months — a consideration that's factored into planning a Bournemouth booking during peak summer dates. Bournemouth's international air college and language schools bring a steady flow of overseas families and students' relatives through the town across the year, and a number of family celebrations here are planned around a visit timed to coincide with a graduation or course completion. Several Bournemouth bookings also tie into a significant wedding anniversary celebrated at the same coastal property year after year, with menu variations planned each time to keep the occasion feeling fresh despite the familiar setting.
