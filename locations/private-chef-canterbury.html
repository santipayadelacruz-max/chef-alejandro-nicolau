Canterbury

Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

Canterbury's cathedral city centre and the substantial houses in the surrounding Kent countryside give the area a character not unlike Winchester's — historic, genuinely well-off, and close enough to London that a private dinner here is a straightforward booking rather than a significant undertaking. Family celebrations and milestone anniversaries held in one of the area's larger period properties make up most bookings, often with a garden well suited to a summer event or a live-cooked paella station. Kent's reputation as the "Garden of England" means genuinely excellent local produce is close at hand — orchards, hop farms, and increasingly a strong English wine industry nearby — and menus for a Canterbury booking often draw on this alongside Alejandro's own broader repertoire. The area's proximity to Sevenoaks and Tunbridge Wells, both already served as part of the wider Home Counties coverage, means a Canterbury booking sometimes connects naturally to a client with a second property or family elsewhere in Kent. The cathedral's own events calendar and the city's university community add a secondary flow of smaller, more academic-tied celebrations too. As with any Kent booking, travel from London is short and logistics are straightforward, with the same full planning conversation covering guest numbers, dietary requirements and format before a menu is confirmed. A number of Canterbury bookings pair the meal itself with a visit to one of the area's growing English vineyards earlier in the day, and where a client wants to build a full day around food and wine, this can be planned as part of the same booking, with a Kent-grown sparkling wine or still wine worked into the evening's pairing. Canterbury's proximity to the Eurotunnel and the Kent coast also makes it a convenient stop for clients travelling between London and continental Europe, and a celebratory dinner here is sometimes planned as part of a longer cross-Channel trip rather than a standalone visit to Kent. A quieter midweek booking is just as available in Canterbury as a weekend celebration, and the same full planning process applies regardless of which day of the week an event falls on.
