Chelsea Harbour

Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

Chelsea Harbour's marina setting, right in the heart of London, makes it one of the more unusual and attractive private dining locations in the capital — residents here often want their events to make the most of the waterside setting, whether that's a cocktail reception overlooking the marina or a full dinner with the boats visible through the window. Alejandro's combined food and bar service suits this setting particularly well, bringing the same attention to drinks as to the menu for an evening that makes full use of the location. Many of the marina's apartments and the Belmond Cadogan-adjacent properties nearby have excellent, well-equipped kitchens despite their compact footprint, and Alejandro's team is experienced at delivering a full multi-course menu from exactly this kind of space. A cocktail reception overlooking the moored boats works particularly well as an opener before guests move to a seated dinner, with the drinks and food menus designed together rather than treated as separate bookings. Reach out via the contact form to discuss the setting and your preferred format. The harbour's private marina berths mean a number of residents keep their own boat moored just steps from their apartment, and a handful of bookings each year combine a short cruise along the river with a return to the apartment for the main meal, treated as a single coordinated evening rather than two separate events. The Design Centre at the heart of Chelsea Harbour also occasionally hosts private trade events, with catering arranged separately through the venue rather than as part of residential bookings. The harbour's proximity to Chelsea proper means several residents also maintain a townhouse nearby, and bookings are sometimes split across both properties within the same week — a formal dinner in the townhouse, a more relaxed drinks evening back at the harbour apartment. The marina's own restaurant row provides a useful point of comparison for clients deciding on a menu style, with Alejandro's team happy to discuss how a private menu can offer something genuinely different from what's already available at the harbour itself. Given the marina setting, weather and tide conditions are occasionally worth factoring into outdoor or boat-based elements of an event, and the team is happy to discuss a sensible indoor backup plan as part of initial planning. Indoor dinners within the apartment itself require no such contingency. Drinks pairings for Chelsea Harbour events are planned with the marina setting in mind, often favouring lighter, more refreshing styles suited to an evening by the water rather than heavier formal pairings. A late-evening nightcap course, such as a small dessert and coffee pairing, suits the relaxed pace many Chelsea Harbour clients prefer for an evening by the marina.
