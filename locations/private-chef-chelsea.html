Chelsea

Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

Chelsea's townhouses along the King's Road and the garden squares behind them are a regular setting for private dinners, family celebrations and the kind of relaxed-but-elevated entertaining the area is known for. Alejandro has cooked extensively for Chelsea clients, from an intimate dinner for a couple to a full Spanish-themed evening with paella cooked fresh in the garden — a popular choice during the warmer months when outdoor entertaining in Chelsea's private gardens comes into its own. The area's proximity to the Chelsea Flower Show season also makes early summer one of the busiest periods for private events here. Beyond the Flower Show, Chelsea's calendar of summer garden parties and the area's strong community of design and fashion professionals bring a confident, visually minded client to private dining here — guests who notice plating and presentation as much as flavour. Alejandro's team works closely with hosts on exactly how a Chelsea garden event should look as well as taste, from table styling cues to the timing of courses around daylight. A short message through the enquiry form with your date and guest count is the easiest way to start planning. Chelsea's townhouses frequently include a lower-ground kitchen that opens directly onto a garden, and this layout suits the kind of indoor-outdoor paella service Alejandro is known for, with the paella pan finished over an open flame just steps from where guests are seated. For clients without garden access, the same Spanish-inspired menu translates comfortably indoors, built around tapas-style sharing plates that suit Chelsea's slightly more relaxed entertaining style. Either format pairs naturally with a cocktail or sangria reception to open the evening. The Chelsea Physic Garden, one of London's oldest botanic gardens, occasionally inspires a herb- and edible-flower-led menu for clients wanting something distinctly tied to the area's own horticultural history. Several King's Road townhouses also retain their original Victorian kitchen layouts even after a full renovation elsewhere in the house, and Alejandro's team is comfortable working within these more traditional, sometimes narrower kitchen footprints without it affecting service. Garden-based Chelsea events, particularly those involving an outdoor paella setup, benefit from a little extra planning time to confirm equipment and any necessary permissions for cooking outdoors. Indoor bookings can typically be arranged with less notice, and the team is happy to advise on which format suits a specific date and guest count best. Allergies and intolerances are planned around carefully from the outset of every Chelsea menu, with cross-contamination risk taken seriously in households catering for a guest with a more significant dietary restriction, not simply substituted at the plate. Fresh bread, baked on the day rather than bought in, is a standard part of most Chelsea menus, consistent with the attention to detail applied across the rest of the meal.
