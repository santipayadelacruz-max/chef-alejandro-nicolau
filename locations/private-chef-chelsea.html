The details

What to expect in Chelsea

01Homes between the King's Road and the river

Chelsea homes are often compact townhouses with kitchens on the lower ground floor and gardens behind. We plan menus around the kitchen layout, finish dishes close to the table and move easily between kitchen and garden room for summer dinners.

02Garden dinners and the summer season

May brings the Chelsea Flower Show and the start of garden season. Menus for al fresco dinners focus on cold starters, raw fish, grilled mains and desserts that hold well outdoors, with the team handling clearing and glassware so you stay with your guests.

03Family life and school-run schedules

Many Chelsea households want dinner served after the children are in bed, or a family lunch that works for all ages. Timings are planned around your household and children's menus are available on request.

04Dinner parties, birthdays and celebrations

Typical bookings are seated dinners for 8 to 16, canapé parties and birthday lunches. A pre-arranged cocktail service from our bar team can run alongside the food for a full evening.

05Cooking for creative households

Chelsea has long been home to artists, designers and collectors, and many households here like menus that feel considered and a little unexpected. We can plan a tasting menu with a theme, a Spanish-inspired dinner or a dinner built around a particular wine.

06A sample spring menu for Chelsea

For a spring dinner: pea and mint velouté with burrata, hand-dived scallop with asparagus and Jersey Royal potatoes, rack of lamb with wild garlic and a rosemary jus, then a rhubarb and vanilla tart. The ingredients follow the market, so the menu is adjusted in the week of the dinner.

Questions

Private Chef in Chelsea: FAQs

Does Alejandro cook for Chelsea Flower Show week parties?

Yes. Dinners and lunches during Flower Show week are popular, so book early to secure the date.

Can you cater a garden dinner in Chelsea?

Yes. Menus can be designed for outdoor service, with cooking done indoors and plated dishes carried out to the table.

Do you provide drinks service in Chelsea?

Yes. Our bar team can create a cocktail menu and serve it for the evening.

Can you plan a themed dinner in Chelsea?

Yes. Spanish, Japanese and other cuisine-led dinners can be planned, including matching drinks.

What is a typical timing for a Chelsea dinner?

Guests are usually welcomed at 7.30pm with canapés, seated at 8pm and finished by 10.30pm, but the timings are set around you.

Can you cook in a Chelsea house with a basement kitchen?

Yes. Basement kitchens are common in Chelsea. We plan the route to the dining room and use warm plates and staged service so dishes arrive at the right temperature.
