Liverpool

Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

Liverpool's waterfront regeneration around the Albert Dock and the grander houses out in Woolton and Allerton give the city two quite different kinds of private dining brief — a sleek apartment dinner with Mersey views on one hand, and a more traditional family celebration in a larger period house on the other. The city's cultural calendar, from its music heritage to a genuinely strong football culture around both Anfield and Goodison, brings a particular flavour to some bookings, with client entertaining and celebratory dinners often timed around a big match weekend or a major event in the city. Liverpool's own food scene has grown considerably, with a strong independent restaurant presence in the Baltic Triangle and around Bold Street, and private bookings here tend to work well precisely because they offer something different from what's already available locally — a seated tasting menu in a client's own home rather than another restaurant booking. As a port city with a long maritime history, Liverpool also has a natural affinity with the wider yacht and coastal side of Alejandro's work, and some clients based here combine a city dinner with a separate coastal or yacht booking elsewhere in the UK. Corporate entertaining is a meaningful part of bookings in Liverpool too, tied to the city's shipping, logistics and growing digital sectors, with the same discretion and professionalism applied to a client dinner here as anywhere else. Travel from London is straightforward by road or rail, and logistics for a Liverpool booking are confirmed as part of the proposal exactly as they would be for any other UK city outside London. Liverpool's relatively compact geography means a visiting chef team can comfortably serve both a city-centre apartment and a house further out in Woolton or Allerton within the same trip, which suits clients who want to host more than one event across a single weekend — a smaller family dinner on one evening, for instance, followed by a larger celebration the next day.
