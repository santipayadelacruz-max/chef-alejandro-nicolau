The details

What to expect in Marylebone

01Terraces, mansion blocks and mews

Marylebone's homes range from Georgian townhouses to apartments with compact kitchens. We confirm layout, equipment and building rules in advance and design menus that work in the space available.

02Dinners for professionals and visitors

With Harley Street and the surrounding offices nearby, hosts here often entertain colleagues and guests from abroad. Small, well-paced dinners for four to ten guests suit this well, with menus that finish on time.

03Local produce and Sunday markets

The Marylebone farmers' market on Sundays shows how much the area cares about good ingredients. Menus lean on seasonal British produce with Spanish and Mediterranean technique.

04Intimate celebrations

Birthdays, anniversaries and dinners for two are all regular bookings. A tasting menu with matched drinks can turn an ordinary evening into something memorable.

05Compact kitchens, serious cooking

Many Marylebone kitchens are compact, which is no obstacle for a tasting menu if it is planned properly. We choose dishes that can be prepped in advance, finished in minutes and plated on warm plates close to the table. The result feels like a restaurant meal in the comfort of your living room.

06A sample lunch menu for Marylebone

A lunch menu might feature crab with avocado and lime, a risotto with seasonal vegetables, grilled halibut with beurre blanc and a lemon tart. It is light enough to leave guests ready for the afternoon.
