Newcastle

Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

Newcastle's Jesmond and Tynemouth areas, with their substantial Victorian and Edwardian houses, are where most larger private dining bookings in the city tend to happen — spacious enough for a proper seated dinner party, and close enough to the quayside and city centre that a visiting chef team has straightforward access. The quayside regeneration itself, with its newer apartment developments overlooking the Tyne, brings a different kind of booking: a smaller, more intimate dinner with a dramatic view as the backdrop rather than a large garden or dining room. Newcastle's identity as a genuinely warm, sociable city comes through in the kind of events typically booked here — family celebrations and milestone birthdays with a slightly less formal, more convivial tone than some of the more buttoned-up corporate bookings further south, though corporate entertaining tied to the city's energy, legal and tech sectors is a steady part of the calendar too. The wider North East, including nearby Durham and the Northumberland coast, occasionally factors into a Newcastle booking when a client has a second property or wants to combine a city dinner with a countryside or coastal stay. As with any northern UK booking, Newcastle sits a reasonable train journey from London, and travel logistics are built into the proposal from the outset. Menus here lean into the same range offered everywhere else — a Spanish-influenced tasting menu, a more classic multi-course fine-dining format, or a relaxed paella-led garden event where space allows — with the specific format and guest count confirmed during the initial planning conversation. Newcastle's genuinely strong sense of civic pride comes through in how often a celebration here is tied to a specific local milestone — a significant work anniversary at a long-standing local company, a retirement after decades in the same role, or simply a family reaching a milestone that the whole extended family wants to mark together in the family home rather than at a restaurant.
