The details

What to expect in Oxfordshire

01A varied county

Oxfordshire's homes range from stone farmhouses and manor houses to riverside villas. We confirm the kitchen and access beforehand and plan menus to suit each one.

02Parkland, palaces and gardens

With Blenheim Palace and its parkland at Woodstock, plus many beautiful private gardens, the county suits garden parties and al fresco dinners. Menus for summer are light and fresh, with raw fish, grilled mains and fruit desserts.

03University connections

With Oxford University's colleges across the county, graduation lunches, reunions and family gatherings are common bookings.

04Weekends and celebrations

A weekend in an Oxfordshire country house may include a Friday supper, a Saturday feast and a relaxed Sunday brunch. We plan the whole weekend as one menu.

05Travel across Oxfordshire

Most of the county is within one to two hours of London. We arrange travel and, where needed, accommodation as part of your booking.

06A sample menu for an Oxfordshire reunion

A family reunion menu might begin with a pea and ham soup, then slow-roasted pork belly with apple sauce and crackling, seasonal vegetables and a baked cheesecake with berries.

Experience

Alejandro’s experience in Oxfordshire

Oxfordshire spans Cotswold-edge villages, parkland and the Thames valley, and the houses vary just as much. Alejandro and his team travel across the county, adapting each menu to the kitchen and the occasion.

Long tables, mixed ages and changing numbers are normal at a weekend house. Years of cooking for private families and international events have taught Alejandro how to keep a menu flexible without losing quality.
