The details

What to expect in Regent's Park

01Nash terraces and park-view apartments

The elegant terraces around the Outer Circle have large rooms and period details, while apartments often have views across the park. We plan menus for each, confirming the kitchen and access in advance.

02Frieze week and autumn entertaining

Frieze London takes place in Regent's Park each October, bringing collectors and visitors. Households here often host dinners around it, so we recommend booking early for that week.

03Summer lunches and garden dinners

In summer, dinners often start on a terrace and move indoors. Menus with raw fish, grilled mains and light desserts suit the season, with canapés served as guests arrive.

04Quiet, private entertaining

Many hosts here value privacy. We arrive early, set up discreetly and clear down quietly after dinner.

05Large houses, grand rooms

Some Regent's Park houses have large reception rooms and formal dining rooms that call for a more ceremonial dinner. We can plan a longer tasting menu with an amuse-bouche, several courses and petit fours, served by a small team. For apartments, the same ambition is scaled to a smaller kitchen.

06A sample autumn menu for Regent's Park

A menu for an autumn evening might feature roast pumpkin soup with amaretti, partridge with bread sauce and Savoy cabbage, a cheese course of British and Spanish cheeses and a baked apple with calvados cream.

Experience

Alejandro’s experience in Regent's Park

Overlooking one of London's grandest parks, a Nash terrace calls for an evening with some sense of occasion. Alejandro keeps the cooking elegant and the service discreet, so the room and the view stay centre stage.

His training under Martín Berasategui, followed by years in private homes and on board yachts, means the evening runs quietly: the host stays at the table and the kitchen is left spotless.
