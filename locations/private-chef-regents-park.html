Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

The grand terraces surrounding Regent's Park, along with the area's private garden squares, offer a particularly elegant backdrop for both indoor dining and garden-based summer entertaining. Alejandro's bookings around Regent's Park often combine a refined tasting menu with the option of outdoor service when weather allows, taking advantage of some of the area's larger private garden spaces. The area's proximity to London Zoo and the park's open-air theatre also makes it a popular base for family celebrations built around a day out nearby. The grand terraces here, many designed by John Nash, often have kitchens tucked into a lower ground floor some distance from the main dining room, and Alejandro's team plans service logistics carefully around that layout so each course arrives at the right temperature. London Zoo's evening events calendar also occasionally overlaps with private Regent's Park bookings, with some households timing a dinner to follow on from an evening visit. A provisional date through the contact form is enough to begin a conversation. The area's famous Nash terraces are Grade I listed, and any catering setup inside them has to respect certain practical constraints around the building's fabric — no open flames beyond what's already provided, careful management of smoke and ventilation — and Alejandro's team is experienced working within these kinds of heritage property restrictions without it affecting the finished menu. Outdoor terrace access on the park-facing side of several properties also makes for a particularly memorable spot for a summer drinks reception. The park's Winter Wonderland and the surrounding area's festive lighting each December bring a particularly busy season for Regent's Park private dining, with several households hosting a Christmas or New Year gathering timed around a visit to the display. ZSL London Zoo's own after-hours private event space is also occasionally booked separately from residential catering, coordinated directly through the zoo's events team rather than as part of a private household booking. Properties around Regent's Park vary considerably in scale, from grand terrace apartments to full townhouses, and an early conversation about the specific kitchen and service space helps the team plan logistics appropriately. Outdoor terrace or garden events benefit from a little extra notice to confirm weather contingencies. A dedicated point of contact manages planning for every Regent's Park booking from first enquiry through to the event itself, so there's never any confusion about who to reach with a last-minute question or change. A pre-dinner canapé course is a popular way to open a Regent's Park evening, giving guests something to enjoy during a drinks reception before the main meal begins.
