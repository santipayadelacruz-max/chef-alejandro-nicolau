Wimbledon

Private dining, cooked exactly where you are.

Wimbledon's leafy streets and large family homes see a particular spike in private entertaining each summer around The Championships, when hosting friends and clients for a day of tennis followed by dinner becomes a seasonal tradition for many local households. Alejandro's Wimbledon bookings reflect this rhythm, with a noticeable increase in enquiries each June and July. Outside of tennis season, the area functions much like other premium London suburbs — family dinners, celebrations, and the occasional larger garden event. Outside the Championships fortnight, the area's large back gardens remain a popular setting for private dining through the rest of the summer, and several Wimbledon clients book a sequence of garden events across the season rather than a single one-off date. The local tennis clubs themselves occasionally host private catering too, separate from household bookings, and Alejandro's team can work within a club's own kitchen facilities when required. Enquiries for either format are welcome through the contact form. Several Wimbledon households with Championships-fortnight bookings host the same group of friends year after year, turning what started as a one-off tennis-watching dinner into an annual tradition, and Alejandro's team keeps notes on previous years' menus specifically so a returning group never gets quite the same dishes twice. Outside the tournament, the area's excellent local butchers and greengrocers along the Village high street provide a convenient source of quality ingredients for more routine family bookings. Beyond the Championships, Wimbledon Common's open heathland hosts a steady calendar of weekend sporting and charity events, and several local bookings each year are timed around a family's participation in one of these, with a celebratory dinner planned for afterwards. The area's strong club culture, including several well-regarded tennis and golf clubs beyond the main Championships venue, also supports a regular pattern of smaller private celebrations tied to club social events throughout the year. Given how far ahead the Championships fortnight books up, an early enquiry is especially worthwhile for anyone planning a tennis-season celebration here. Outside that specific window, Wimbledon bookings follow a standard, more flexible planning timeline similar to the rest of South West London. For Championships-fortnight bookings involving a full day of televised tennis, meal timing is planned around match schedules so food never competes with key moments on court. A light, refreshing starter suits the summer timing of most Championships-fortnight Wimbledon bookings, setting the right tone before a more substantial main course.
