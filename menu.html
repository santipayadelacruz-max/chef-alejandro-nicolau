Spanish Heritage

A Taste of Home

Alongside the fine-dining menu, Alejandro keeps a close connection to Spain, working with Álvaro to bring authentic Spanish cooking to the table: proper paella, real tapas, and the dishes he grew up with.

It's a different register to the tasting menus above: bigger platters, meant for sharing, built for a table of friends or a full celebration.

Paella Master

Álvaro's Paella

Álvaro cooks paella the traditional way, proper socarrat, cooked to order, alongside a spread of authentic Spanish tapas. It's a brilliant, informal option for larger gatherings, garden parties or a relaxed alternative to a plated menu.
