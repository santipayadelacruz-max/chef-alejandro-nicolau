Peruvian Private Chef

Ceviche, Nikkei & coastal Peru

Bright, citrus-led Peruvian cooking — proper ceviche and the Japanese-Peruvian Nikkei tradition, built on exceptional fresh seafood.

Enquire Now Explore the Menus

Peruvian cuisine has become one of the most celebrated culinary traditions in fine dining over the past two decades, and for good reason — a genuine fusion of indigenous ingredients, Spanish colonial influence and a distinctive Japanese-Peruvian Nikkei tradition born from a wave of 19th-century immigration. Alejandro's Peruvian menus centre on this bright, citrus-forward style, built around properly sourced fresh seafood and the precise acid balance that defines a well-made ceviche.

Peruvian private chef: key facts

What to expect

01Ceviche, done properly

A well-made ceviche depends entirely on the quality of the fish and the precision of the citrus marinade, and this is treated as a genuine centrepiece dish rather than a token starter — fresh, properly sourced and balanced on the day.

02The Nikkei tradition

Peru's Nikkei cuisine, blending Japanese technique with Peruvian ingredients, gives the menu a distinctive register of its own — a natural bridge for guests who enjoy both Japanese and South American cooking within the same evening.

03Beyond ceviche

Peruvian cuisine extends well beyond ceviche alone, and a full tasting menu can include richer, Andean-influenced dishes alongside the coastal, citrus-forward courses, giving a genuinely complete picture of the country's cooking.

04What's included

A Peruvian booking includes menu planning covering ceviche and the wider tasting menu, all shopping and sourcing of fresh seafood, on-site preparation and service, and a complete clean-up before the team leaves.

05Suited to a warm-weather celebration

Peruvian cuisine's bright, citrus-forward style makes it a particularly good fit for a summer garden event or a celebration that calls for something fresh and distinctive.

06Pisco & drinks pairing

A Peruvian menu pairs naturally with pisco-based cocktails, and this can be planned as part of the same booking through the existing Bar & Cocktail Hire service where a client wants a fuller drinks pairing.

07A menu for a distinctive celebration

Peruvian cuisine's relative rarity in UK private dining makes it a popular choice for a host who wants their event to feel genuinely distinctive rather than following a more familiar format.

08A distinctive dessert course

Peruvian desserts, often built around native ingredients like purple corn or lucuma, give the end of the meal the same sense of discovery as the courses before it, rather than defaulting to something more generic.

09Booking a Peruvian menu

An enquiry through the contact form, mentioning your date and guest count, starts the planning conversation, with a full proposal confirmed within 24 hours.

10Scaling to your event

A Peruvian menu is planned to suit any guest count, from an intimate ceviche-led dinner through to a larger celebration, with the kitchen team scaled accordingly.

11Equipment & setup

The team brings any specialist equipment a Peruvian menu calls for, so a standard home kitchen is all that's needed from the host, including for a ceviche-led menu.

12A menu for any time of year

While particularly suited to a summer event, a Peruvian menu works well year-round, with the balance between lighter ceviche courses and richer Andean dishes adjusted to suit the season.

13Drinks to match

A pisco sour alongside the earlier ceviche courses, moving to something with more body for the Andean dishes that follow, is a natural pairing structure, and Alejandro is happy to suggest one suited to your specific menu.

14How it's presented

Ceviche and other raw-fish courses are dressed and plated to order, right before serving, so the citrus-cured fish reaches the table at exactly the right point of freshness.

15Make it your own

The balance between ceviche-led, ocean-focused courses and heartier Andean dishes such as lomo saltado is adjusted to your own preferences, so the menu reflects the version of Peruvian cooking you actually want on the night.

16Peruvian flavours, carefully balanced

Peruvian cooking combines native ingredients with Japanese, Spanish and Chinese influences, which is why it feels both familiar and exciting. Ceviche is made to order with the freshest fish, cut and dressed just before serving in a bright citrus marinade. Alongside it come causas, anticuchos and slow-cooked stews, with ají peppers adding warmth and depth rather than just heat.

17A menu that moves from light to rich

A Peruvian dinner often starts with raw and cured fish, moves to warm, comforting dishes such as lomo saltado or a seafood rice, and finishes with a dessert using lucuma or purple corn. This progression gives the evening shape and keeps the food varied. If you have eaten Peruvian food in a restaurant and loved a particular dish, mention it and it can be built into your menu.

Questions

Peruvian Private Chef: FAQs

Is ceviche the main focus of the menu, or just one course?

Ceviche is typically a centrepiece, but Alejandro Nicolau's Peruvian tasting menu extends well beyond it, including richer Andean-influenced dishes.

What is Nikkei cuisine, and is it part of the menu?

Nikkei is Peru's Japanese-influenced culinary tradition, blending Japanese technique with Peruvian ingredients. It is a natural and popular part of a Peruvian menu from Alejandro Nicolau.

Is the seafood sourced fresh specifically for the booking?

Yes. Because fresh, properly sourced seafood is central to Peruvian cooking, Alejandro Nicolau plans the sourcing around your specific date.

Can a Peruvian menu work well alongside guests who also enjoy Japanese food?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau's Peruvian menu suits a table that also enjoys Japanese food, because the Nikkei tradition bridges the two cuisines naturally.
