A dinner party should mean sitting at the table with your guests, not disappearing into the kitchen between courses. Booking a private chef for a dinner party means handing over exactly that part of the evening — the shopping, the prep, the cooking and the plating — while you focus entirely on your guests. Menus are built around your specific group: a relaxed dinner for six close friends looks very different from a more formal evening for twelve, and both are planned with the same care.

What's included

A dinner party booking typically includes a full planning conversation to confirm your menu, all shopping and ingredient sourcing, cooking on-site in your own kitchen, plated or family-style service for each course, and a complete clean-up before the team leaves — your kitchen left exactly as it was found.

Choosing a menu

Menus range from a focused three-course dinner to a full multi-course tasting experience, and can draw on any of Alejandro's cuisines — Mediterranean, Asian, South American, Spanish heritage menus, or a fusion built around your own favourite dishes. Dietary requirements across the table, from a simple intolerance to a more complex set of needs, are planned around as standard.

Scaling to your group

Dinner parties are booked for anywhere from two guests to twenty or more, with the kitchen team scaled to match — a smaller, more intimate dinner needs less support than a larger seated celebration, and this is confirmed as part of your initial enquiry.

A typical evening, start to finish

The team usually arrives several hours ahead of guests to set up and begin prep, keeping the kitchen calm and organised well before the first course is due. Courses are then cooked and plated in sequence through the evening, timed around your own pace rather than a fixed schedule, so a conversation that runs long between courses never leaves anyone rushed in the kitchen.

Wine & drinks alongside the menu

While the core booking is built around food, a wine pairing or a simple drinks service can be added to the same evening — either suggested alongside each course or kept deliberately informal, depending on how the rest of the evening is planned. This is confirmed during the same planning conversation as the food menu itself.

Booking for a special occasion vs. a regular catch-up

Some dinner party bookings mark a specific occasion — a birthday, a promotion, a reunion with friends who rarely get a free evening together — while others are simply a standing arrangement for a group that likes to eat well together every month or so. Both are planned with the same attention, though a milestone occasion often prompts a slightly more ambitious menu, discussed as part of the initial enquiry.

Setting the scene

A private chef dinner party works best when the table, lighting and pace are planned alongside the menu. Alejandro Nicolau is happy to advise on how courses should be timed around conversation, when to serve drinks and how to move guests from a welcome course to the table without disrupting the evening.

From arrival to final course

On the day, the team arrives early, sets up the kitchen, cooks and plates each course, serves the table and leaves your kitchen clean. You stay with your guests throughout, which is the point of hiring a private chef for a dinner party in the first place.
