Private Chef for a Week

Extended stay & multi-day service

A full week of meals, planned as one coherent experience rather than a series of separate bookings.

Enquire Now Meal Prep Service

Booking a private chef for an extended stay — a week in London, a holiday house abroad, or an extended family gathering — means every meal across the visit is planned together, so nothing feels repetitive from one day to the next. It's a different kind of planning from a single dinner booking: breakfasts, lunches and dinners are varied deliberately across the week, built around what the household actually wants to eat each day rather than a fixed, generic menu.

What's included in a week-long booking

A full week typically includes daily menu planning and shopping, cooking for whichever meals you'd like covered — some households want every meal handled, others just dinners — and a flexible approach to any changes in plans across the stay, such as a guest arriving later in the week or a day trip that shifts mealtimes.

Suited to any setting

This format works equally well for a London residence, a countryside house for a longer weekend, or a holiday let abroad, with ingredients and local sourcing adapted to wherever the stay is based. For properties outside the UK, travel and provisioning logistics are confirmed as part of the proposal.

A trial before committing further

Many households use a single week-long booking as a way to try an ongoing relationship with Alejandro before deciding whether to continue with a longer-term, regular arrangement — there's no obligation to commit beyond the initial stay.

Grocery sourcing & dietary consistency

Shopping for a full week is planned as one exercise rather than a series of daily trips, which keeps ingredients fresher and the kitchen better organised across the stay. Any dietary requirements, allergies or simple preferences flagged at the start are then carried consistently through every meal for the week, rather than needing to be repeated each day.

Working around a household's own schedule

Extended bookings are built to flex around a household's actual rhythm — an early breakfast before a day of activities, a late dinner after guests arrive from the airport, or a quiet lunch left ready for whenever it suits. The initial conversation covers your household's typical pattern so the week is planned around real life rather than a fixed restaurant-style schedule.

A sample shape for a typical week

No two weeks look identical, but a common pattern includes a lighter breakfast and lunch most days, with dinner as the main planned event, perhaps with one or two more elaborate evenings built in for a birthday, an anniversary falling during the stay, or simply a Saturday night the household wants to make special — all agreed during the initial planning conversation rather than fixed in advance.

Settling into your routine

On the first day Alejandro Nicolau learns how your household eats: when breakfast happens, who is out for lunch and which evenings you want something special. The plan then follows your routine rather than the other way round, so the week feels effortless instead of scheduled.

Looking after guests who visit

Over a week, friends and family often drop in. Alejandro Nicolau plans a little flexibility into each day's menu, so an unexpected guest at lunch or an extra person at dinner can be welcomed without disrupting the plan or the standard of the food.

Shopping, storage and leftovers

Ingredients are bought fresh for the week and stored carefully, with portions planned to avoid waste. Where there are leftovers, they are reworked into lunches or the next day's dishes, so the food stays fresh and nothing is thrown away unnecessarily.
