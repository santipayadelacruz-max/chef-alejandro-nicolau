What to expect

01Built around your training

Meals are planned around your specific training schedule and goals — whether that's a strength-focused macro split, a cutting phase ahead of a competition, or simply consistent, high-quality fuel through a demanding training block.

02Macro-precise, without feeling clinical

Protein, carbohydrate and fat targets are built into every meal, calculated and tracked where needed, without the meals themselves feeling like a diet plan — proper seasoning, real variety, and dishes worth looking forward to.

03Weekly planning & delivery

A rotating weekly menu is planned around your macros, with meals prepared and delivered or cooked fresh depending on your preference, adjusted as your training phase or goals change.

04Working with your own coach or nutritionist

Where an athlete already has a coach, nutritionist or performance team setting specific targets, the kitchen works directly from those figures rather than setting independent goals — meal plans are built to hit whatever macro and calorie targets your own team has already defined.

05Competition-day & travel considerations

Nutrition in the days immediately before a competition, and food that needs to travel well to an away event or training camp, both call for slightly different planning than a normal training week, and this is factored in specifically when a competition date or away trip is flagged in advance.

06Supplements & whole-food priorities

The emphasis throughout is on hitting nutritional targets through properly cooked, whole-food meals rather than leaning on supplementation to make up a shortfall — supplements remain the athlete's or their nutritionist's own decision, with the kitchen focused on making the food itself do as much of the work as possible.

07Suited to individual athletes & small training groups

This service works equally well for a single athlete managing their own training and for a small group — training partners or a family where several people share similar performance goals — with menus planned to suit the group as a whole where appropriate.

08Flexible delivery around a training schedule

Meal timing and delivery are planned around actual training hours rather than a fixed daily schedule, so food arrives ready to eat around sessions rather than requiring the athlete to work their day around a kitchen's own timetable.

09Shopping & ingredient quality

Ingredient sourcing follows the same standard applied across all of Alejandro's menus — properly sourced protein and fresh produce, rather than the mass-produced convenience ingredients common in many standard meal-prep services, so performance meals never feel like a compromise on quality.

10Eating around training and travel

Training schedules rarely fit a fixed meal plan. Alejandro Nicolau plans meals around your sessions, rest days and travel, with options that can be eaten cold, reheated or taken on the road when needed. The result is food that supports performance without taking over your week.

11Learning from feedback

After the first week, the menu is refined using your feedback on energy, appetite and recovery. Portions, seasoning and ingredients are adjusted so the plan keeps working and does not become repetitive over a long training block.

Questions

Private Chef for Athletes: FAQs

Can meals be planned around a specific macro split for training or competition prep?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau builds meals around your protein, carbohydrate and fat targets for training or competition prep, and adjusts them as your training phase changes.

Will the food still taste good, or is it a strict diet-style menu?

The aim is genuinely enjoyable food that meets precise nutritional targets. Alejandro Nicolau's athlete menus have proper seasoning and real variety, not a bland diet plan.

Can the menu change as I move through different training phases?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau adjusts the menu as your goals shift, whether you are in a bulking phase, a cut or maintenance.

Is this service available as weekly meal prep, or only as fresh daily cooking?

Both. Alejandro Nicolau offers weekly prepared meals, or a chef cooking fresh on a daily or several-times-a-week basis, depending on what suits you.
