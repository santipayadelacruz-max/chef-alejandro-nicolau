An intimate dinner — for two, four or six guests — is sometimes treated as a smaller, simpler version of a bigger event, but it deserves exactly the opposite approach: with fewer guests to focus on, every detail gets more attention, not less. Alejandro's intimate dinner service is built around this idea, whether it's a quiet anniversary dinner for two or a close friends' gathering for six.

The details

What to expect

01Why smaller doesn't mean simpler

A smaller guest list means more time and attention per course, more room for a genuinely bespoke menu, and the chance to tailor every detail — from pacing to presentation — to exactly the two or four or six people at the table.

02A fully plated tasting menu, at your table

Intimate dinners are particularly well suited to a full multi-course tasting menu, plated and served course by course, in a way that would be harder to coordinate at a much larger table.

03Special occasions, quietly done

Anniversaries, proposals, milestone birthdays shared with only the closest friends — intimate dinners are often tied to a significant personal occasion, and planned with the discretion and attention that deserves.

04Pacing a smaller tasting menu properly

With fewer guests to serve, course pacing can be judged much more precisely — reading the table's own rhythm rather than working to a fixed schedule, so a longer conversation between courses never forces the kitchen to rush, and a quieter table is never left waiting.

05A quieter, more personal style of service

Service for a smaller group is naturally lower-key than for a larger event — less visible staff presence, a quieter kitchen, and a style of hosting built around a handful of guests rather than a room, which suits an evening meant to feel genuinely private.

06A natural fit for a milestone just for two

Where a larger party doesn't suit the occasion — a quiet anniversary, a private celebration between partners, or simply an evening two people want entirely to themselves — an intimate dinner gives exactly that scale of event the same calibre of cooking as a much bigger celebration.

07A lower-key alternative to a larger party

For hosts who want to mark an occasion without the scale and logistics of a full party, an intimate dinner offers a genuine alternative — all the care and quality of a larger celebration, scaled down to a size that feels right for a smaller, closer group.

08Seasonal & tasting-menu formats

Intimate dinners work particularly well as a seasonal tasting menu, built around whatever is best at the time of year, since a smaller group makes it easier to plan a longer sequence of smaller courses than would suit a larger table.

09Choosing the right room or setting at home

Even within a larger home, an intimate dinner is often set in a smaller, more considered room rather than a main dining room built for bigger gatherings — a choice discussed together so the setting matches the scale and feel of the evening.

10Designing for a smaller table

Intimate dinners allow every detail to be considered: the order of courses, the pace between them, the table and the lighting. Alejandro Nicolau plans each element with you, so the evening feels personal and unhurried.

11Discretion and atmosphere

For occasions such as proposals and anniversaries, the team is quiet, attentive and unobtrusive. Plating is done in the kitchen, service is timed to the conversation and the chef appears only when it adds to the evening.

12An evening for two or a handful of guests

Intimate dinners are about the details: a candlelit table, a menu planned around your tastes and service that feels invisible. Alejandro cooks in your kitchen, so the food arrives fresh and the evening feels private. Anniversaries, proposals and quiet celebrations are common occasions.

13Menus with a sense of occasion

A tasting menu of four to seven courses is often the best fit, with each plate small and carefully made. Pairings, flowers and music can be arranged around the food if you wish. The idea is that you spend the evening together, and the dinner takes care of itself.

14Where we cook

Alejandro cooks in private homes and venues across London, including Mayfair, Kensington, Chelsea, Knightsbridge, Belgravia, Notting Hill, Marylebone, Hampstead, St John's Wood, Wimbledon. He also travels for events across the United Kingdom, including Surrey, Windsor, Henley-on-Thames, the Cotswolds, Oxford, Cambridge, Brighton, Bath, Edinburgh, Manchester.
