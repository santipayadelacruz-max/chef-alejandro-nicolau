Paella cooked properly is a live event in itself — the pan finished in front of guests, the socarrat (the prized crisped rice at the base) achieved only by watching the pan closely over real heat. Alejandro's paella catering brings exactly that experience to a private garden, terrace or indoor event in London, trained in the technique through his background in Spanish fine dining.

What to expect

01How it works

A paella station is set up at your event — outdoors in a garden where space allows, or adapted for an indoor kitchen — with the dish cooked fresh in front of guests rather than prepared ahead and reheated. This makes it a natural centrepiece for a garden party, a summer celebration or a relaxed Spanish-themed evening.

02Paella styles available

Traditional options include a classic Valencian paella, a seafood-led paella marinera, and a mixed paella combining meat and seafood; vegetarian versions are equally available on request, each built around properly sourced bomba rice and saffron.

03Beyond the paella itself

A paella event is often paired with Spanish tapas or pintxos to start, and a glass of sangria or a simple Spanish wine pairing — built into the same booking as a complete Spanish-themed evening rather than a single dish on its own.

04Guest numbers & serving style

Paella scales naturally to larger groups in a way some other live-cooked dishes don't, making it a popular choice for garden parties and larger summer gatherings. Depending on numbers, the dish is served either plated at the table or set out buffet-style straight from the pan, with the format agreed as part of planning the event.

05Pairing paella with a wider Spanish menu

For hosts who want a fuller Spanish-themed event rather than a single dish, paella pairs naturally with the wider Spanish heritage menu — starting with tapas or pintxos, moving through the paella as a centrepiece, and finishing with a simple Spanish dessert such as crema catalana.

06Weather contingency for outdoor events

An outdoor paella setup depends on reasonable weather, and a simple contingency — moving the station under cover or adapting to an indoor kitchen at short notice — is agreed as part of planning any garden-based event, so a change in forecast never puts the booking itself at risk.

07Equipment brought to your event

A proper paella pan, the right burner setup, and all other equipment needed to cook the dish over real heat are brought as standard — nothing beyond outdoor space and access for setup is required from the host.

08A relaxed, sociable style of cooking

Because the dish is cooked in full view of guests rather than hidden away in a kitchen, a paella station naturally becomes a sociable focal point for the event — guests tend to gather around and watch the process, which suits a relaxed garden party far better than a formal plated service.

09A centrepiece for the evening

Paella is as much a show as a meal. Guests gather as the rice is cooked, the aromas build and the dish is brought to the table in its pan. Alejandro Nicolau times the cooking so that it arrives at its best, with the socarrat crisp and the rice just right.

10Choosing the right paella for your guests

Traditional options include seafood, mixed and vegetable paellas, and the choice depends on your guests and the season. Alejandro Nicolau can advise on quantities, side dishes and drinks, so the evening feels generous without leaving too much to waste.

Private Chef Paella Catering: FAQs

Is the paella cooked fresh on-site, or prepared in advance?

Cooked fresh, on site, in front of your guests. Alejandro Nicolau's live paella cooking, including achieving a proper socarrat, is central to the experience.

Can paella be cooked indoors if I don't have outdoor space?

Yes. Outdoor paella is popular in good weather, but Alejandro Nicolau can cook the dish comfortably in an indoor kitchen too.

Is a vegetarian paella option available?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau can prepare a vegetarian paella alongside, or instead of, the traditional meat and seafood versions.

Can paella be combined with other Spanish dishes for a full themed evening?

Yes. Tapas, pintxos and a Spanish wine or sangria pairing are commonly added to Alejandro Nicolau's paella to build a complete Spanish-themed evening.
