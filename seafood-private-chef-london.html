Fish and seafood reward careful sourcing and precise cooking. Alejandro cooks seafood menus at home using the best available catch, from raw crudo and ceviche to roast whole fish and shellfish tasting menus, trained in the Basque tradition.

A seafood menu suits summer dinners, pescatarian guests and celebrations. For paella, see the paella page; for a lighter Mediterranean menu, see the Mediterranean page.

Seafood & Fish: key facts

Alejandro Nicolau is a London-based private chef who offers seafood & fish cooked fresh in the client's own home or venue, with shopping, cooking, service and the kitchen left clean, across London and the United Kingdom.

Tips

Seafood tips

01Buy fresh

Fish is bought close to the day for best flavour.

02Keep it simple

Good fish needs little adornment.

03Serve cold things cold

Raw and cured dishes need a good chill.

04Pair with white wine

Crisp whites and sparkling work best.

Questions

Seafood & Fish Private Chef: FAQs

Can you cook a seafood tasting menu at home?

Yes. Seafood tasting menus are a regular request.

Do you serve raw fish?

Yes. Crudo and ceviche are on the menu, always made from fresh fish.

Can you cook for guests with shellfish allergies?

Yes, with careful planning. See our allergy-friendly page.

Where does the fish come from?

Ingredients are sourced for quality and freshness for each booking. Ask about what is in season.

Do you cook seafood paella?

Yes. See the paella catering page.
