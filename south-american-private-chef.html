South America's culinary traditions span an enormous range, from Argentina's confident, fire-driven asado culture to the bright, citrus-forward ceviche of the Pacific coast, and a South American menu here draws on this full breadth rather than settling on a single narrow style. Alejandro's South American menus are planned around genuine regional technique, built to suit whichever part of the continent's cooking a client wants to explore.

The details

What to expect

01Asado & proper grilling

Argentine and wider South American grilling technique — proper cuts, real fire, patient cooking rather than a rushed sear — forms the backbone of many South American menus, bringing genuine depth of flavour to the meat course.

02Coastal & citrus-forward dishes

Alongside the grill, lighter coastal dishes built around fresh seafood and citrus provide genuine contrast across a South American tasting menu, balancing the richer grilled courses with something brighter.

03A menu that can draw on Peru specifically

Where a client wants to focus more specifically on Peruvian cooking within a South American evening, dishes can draw more heavily from the dedicated Peruvian menu as part of the same booking.

04What's included

A South American booking includes menu planning across grilled and coastal courses, all shopping and sourcing, on-site cooking — including proper grill technique where space allows — and a complete clean-up before the team leaves.

05Suited to an outdoor celebration

The cuisine's confident, fire-driven cooking style makes it a particularly strong fit for a summer garden event with a live grill station as a central feature.

06Wine & drinks pairing

South American wine, particularly from Argentina and Chile, pairs naturally with a grill-led menu, and a pairing can be planned alongside the food as part of the same booking.

07A menu for a confident, social evening

The cuisine's bold, communal character — built around a shared grill and generous portions — suits a lively, social gathering particularly well, encouraging guests to linger at the table.

08A rich, confident dessert

South American desserts bring the same boldness as the rest of the menu — a proper dulce de leche-based dish, or something built around tropical fruit from further up the continent, chosen to finish the meal with real character.

09Booking a South American menu

An enquiry through the contact form, with your date, guest count and any country-specific preference, starts the planning process, with a full proposal confirmed within 24 hours.

10Scaling to your event

A South American menu is planned to suit any guest count, from a small dinner through to a larger garden celebration built around a live grill station.

11Equipment & setup

Where a live grill isn't possible, grilled dishes are adapted to a standard kitchen setup, so a booking remains fully possible even without outdoor space.

12A menu for any time of year

While an outdoor grill suits the summer months particularly well, a South American menu adapts comfortably to an indoor setting at any time of year.

13Drinks to match

A South American menu pairs well with the region's own wines — a bold Malbec alongside grilled meats, something lighter for ceviche or ocean-forward courses — and Alejandro is glad to suggest a structure to suit your specific menu.

14How it's presented

Grilled and smoked dishes are plated with the same bold, confident style the asado tradition is known for, while raw and citrus-cured courses are finished more delicately, right before serving.

15Make it your own

Which countries the menu draws from most heavily, and the balance between grilled, smoked and raw courses, is discussed with you in advance, so the final menu reflects your own taste rather than a single fixed format.

16A continent of flavours

South American cuisine ranges from Argentine grilled meats and Brazilian feijoada to Colombian arepas, Chilean seafood and Peruvian ceviche. Menus can focus on one country or draw on several, using fire, citrus, chilli and herbs. Live-fire cooking and slow braises give the food depth and warmth, and the style is generous and welcoming.

17Parties, sharing and live grills

South American food suits relaxed, social events. Grilled meats and fish can be served in generous sharing portions, alongside salads, sauces such as chimichurri and sides built for the table. A cocktail such as a pisco sour or caipirinha from the bar service adds to the atmosphere. Vegetarian guests are looked after with grilled vegetables, corn-based dishes and hearty salads.

18Where we cook

Alejandro cooks in private homes and venues across London, including Mayfair, Kensington, Chelsea, Knightsbridge, Belgravia, Notting Hill, Marylebone, Hampstead, St John's Wood, Wimbledon. He also travels for events across the United Kingdom, including Surrey, Windsor, Henley-on-Thames, the Cotswolds, Oxford, Cambridge, Brighton, Bath, Edinburgh, Manchester. Questions

South American Private Chef: FAQs

Does the menu include a proper Argentine-style grill course?

Yes. A proper asado-style grilled course, cooked over real fire with patient technique, is a common centrepiece of Alejandro Nicolau's South American menu.

Can the menu focus more specifically on one country's cuisine, like Argentina or Peru?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau can focus a South American menu on one country, such as Argentina or Peru, drawing on his dedicated Peruvian menu where relevant.

Is there a lighter, seafood-based option alongside the grilled dishes?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau plans coastal, citrus-forward dishes alongside the richer grilled courses so the menu has balance and contrast.

Is this menu suited to an outdoor event with a real grill or fire?

Yes, where the property has suitable outdoor space. A live grill station makes a strong centrepiece for a garden-based South American event cooked by Alejandro Nicolau.
