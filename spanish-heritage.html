Spanish Heritage

A Taste of Home

Alejandro was born in Spain in 1998, and trained in San Sebastián in the Basque Country before joining Martín Berasategui's three-Michelin-star kitchen. Fine dining is where his career started, but the food he grew up with, and still cooks at home, is a different thing entirely.

Working alongside Álvaro, he brings that side of Spain to private events in London and beyond: authentic paella, real tapas, and the dishes that don't usually make it onto a tasting menu.

Tapas & Paella Dining

A Different Way to Eat

Rather than a plated tasting menu, tapas and paella dining is built around the table: a spread of small plates to start, patatas bravas, jamón, croquetas, pan con tomate, gambas al ajillo, followed by paella cooked to order in front of your guests.

It's informal by design: ideal for gardens, larger groups, birthdays, or anything that calls for good food and easy conversation over a long, shared table rather than a formal service.

Paella Master

Álvaro's Paella

Álvaro cooks paella the traditional way, proper socarrat, cooked to order, alongside a spread of authentic Spanish tapas. It's a brilliant, informal option for larger gatherings, garden parties or a relaxed alternative to a plated menu.
