Vegan Private Chef

Plant-based, without compromise

A fully plant-based tasting menu that stands entirely on its own — not a reduced version of a standard menu with the meat removed.

A good vegan menu isn't a smaller version of a standard menu with the meat and dairy simply removed — it's planned from the ground up, with technique and flavour built specifically around what plants do best. Alejandro's vegan tasting menus are developed with exactly this approach, aiming for genuinely restaurant-quality results rather than a compromise, whether for a fully vegan table or a guest list that's mostly not.

The details

What to expect

01Built from the ground up

Dishes are planned specifically as vegan courses rather than adapted from an existing meat-based menu, using proper technique — fermentation, careful seasoning, varied texture — to build genuine depth without relying on animal products.

02Suited to a mixed guest list

A vegan menu works just as well for a fully plant-based table as for a mixed guest list where only some guests are vegan — the same menu is planned to be genuinely satisfying for everyone at the table, not treated as a separate side menu.

03Seasonal, produce-led cooking

Vegan cooking leans naturally into seasonal produce, and menus here change through the year to make the most of whatever's genuinely at its best, rather than relying on a handful of fixed ingredients year-round.

04What's included

A vegan booking includes a full planning conversation covering your preferred style and any specific requests, all shopping and sourcing of quality plant-based ingredients, on-site cooking and service, and a complete clean-up before the team leaves.

05Suited to any occasion

A vegan menu is planned to suit any occasion, from an intimate dinner for two through to a larger celebration, with the same attention to detail and presentation as any other menu.

06Technique over substitution

Rather than leaning on processed meat substitutes, dishes are built around proper technique — fermentation, roasting, careful use of texture and umami — to create genuine depth without needing a like-for-like replacement for meat or dairy.

07Suited to a dietary-conscious celebration

A fully vegan menu is a popular choice for a milestone celebration where the host wants a genuinely thoughtful, modern menu that happens to be plant-based, rather than treating veganism as a limitation to work around.

08Dessert, without compromise

Vegan dessert is planned with the same rigour as the rest of the menu — proper technique rather than a simple substitution — so the meal finishes with something genuinely satisfying rather than a noticeably lesser course.

09Booking a vegan menu

An enquiry through the contact form, with your date, guest count and any specific requests, starts the planning conversation, with a full proposal confirmed within 24 hours.

10Equipment & setup

No specialist equipment is required from the host for a vegan menu — the team brings everything needed, exactly as for any other cuisine.

11A menu for any occasion

A vegan menu is just as suited to an everyday family dinner as to a significant milestone celebration, with the same level of care applied regardless of the occasion's scale.

12Wine to match

A vegan tasting menu is paired with wine in exactly the same way as any other — the pairing is built around the dish's weight, acidity and seasoning rather than treated as a separate consideration, and Alejandro can suggest a structure to suit your own cellar.

13How it's presented

Plating is given the same attention as any other tasting menu — texture and colour are used deliberately, so a vegan course reads as a complete, considered dish rather than a lighter stand-in for something else.

14Plant-based cooking with real depth

A good vegan menu is not a meat dish with the meat removed. It is built from vegetables, grains, pulses, nuts and herbs treated with the same technique and care as any fine-dining plate. Depth of flavour comes from roasting, fermenting, smoking and slow reduction, and texture comes from contrast between crisp, creamy and tender elements. Even guests who do not usually choose plant-based food tend to ask for seconds.

15Cooking for mixed tables

Many hosts are cooking for a table where only one or two guests are vegan. A vegan chef can design a menu where everyone eats the same dishes, or where the plant-based plates sit alongside others without feeling like an afterthought. Dessert is treated with equal care, using fruit, chocolate, nuts and plant-based creams. Allergies and other dietary needs are planned in at the same time.

16Where we cook

Alejandro cooks in private homes and venues across London, including Mayfair, Kensington, Chelsea, Knightsbridge, Belgravia, Notting Hill, Marylebone, Hampstead, St John's Wood, Wimbledon. He also travels for events across the United Kingdom, including Surrey, Windsor, Henley-on-Thames, the Cotswolds, Oxford, Cambridge, Brighton, Bath, Edinburgh, Manchester. Questions

Vegan Private Chef: FAQs

Is the vegan menu a modified version of a standard menu, or built separately?

Built separately. Alejandro Nicolau designs a vegan menu as vegan from the outset, using techniques suited to plant-based cooking, rather than removing animal products from an existing dish.

Can a vegan menu work for a guest list where only some guests are vegan?

Yes. Alejandro Nicolau plans a vegan menu to satisfy the whole table, so it works when only some guests are vegan and is not treated as a lesser option.

Does the vegan menu change with the seasons?

Yes. Like the rest of Alejandro Nicolau's menus, vegan dishes are planned around genuinely seasonal produce and vary through the year.

Is this different from the existing Meal Prep service?

Yes. A vegan private chef dinner is a tasting-menu format for an event, while Meal Prep covers weekly prepared meals; Alejandro Nicolau offers a vegan approach within either on request.
